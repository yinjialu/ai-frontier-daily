We’re launching Lyria 3.5 in Google Flow Music, with advances across musicality, lyrics, vocals, and creative control.
Jul 29, 2026
Our newest music generation model, Lyria 3.5, delivers significant advancements across musicality, lyrics, and vocal quality, empowering you to craft richer tracks. We’re rolling it out today in
Google Flow Music
, where we want to help you create songs you love, with creative control.
Here's what’s new:
Improved musicality:
Create richer, more complex melodic structures that sound more natural.
Enhanced lyrics:
Generate higher quality lyrics with improved prompt adherence and structural awareness.
Improved vocals:
Bring more expression and emotion to your songs with more realistic and emotionally nuanced vocals, plus improved pronunciation.
Creative control:
More easily control the tempo and duration of your outputs.
Try it today in
Flow Music
.
