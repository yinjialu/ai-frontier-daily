Simplify your morning with this vibe-coded schedule app.
Jul 31, 2026
Tired of starting your morning staring at bright screens and stressful notifications? Raph, a creative technologist at Google, built a custom app called Glanceboard with Gemini 3.6 Flash and Nano Banana to transform that daily routine.
Every morning, Glanceboard checks Raph’s family Google Calendar and the local weather. Then, it prompts Nana Banana to generate a personalized illustration of his children wearing forecast-friendly clothes. That image shows up on an e-ink display, letting Raphael’s family know their schedule, what to wear, and what to pack for the day.
Glanceboard operates on a lightweight local server — no cloud accounts or bright screens required — offering a serene and personalized way to stay organized. And Raphael says it’s a great way to get kids engaged and organized without the stress.
Want to try Glanceboard yourself? We’re
open-sourcing the code
so you can ask Google Antigravity to make you one, too. Get the full details on how to build it and the hardware he used in the GitHub repo.
