Keep your SAT prep on track with practice tests in Gemini.
Aug 18, 2026
If you’re busy studying for the SAT, you’re in good company — “SAT prep,” “SAT prep websites,” and “SAT preparation classes near me” have all become breakout Google searches over the past three months, according to Google Trends data. The good news: You can take full-length, on-demand practice
SAT tests in the Gemini app
at no cost. Simply tell Gemini, “
I want to take a practice SAT test.
”
Launched earlier this year, these practice tests are grounded in rigorously vetted content from
The Princeton Review
. This helps ensure you’re preparing with material that more closely resembles what you’ll see on test day.
When you complete a practice test in Gemini, you’ll receive immediate feedback highlighting where you excelled and where you might need to study more. For anything you don’t understand, just ask Gemini to explain the correct answer.
