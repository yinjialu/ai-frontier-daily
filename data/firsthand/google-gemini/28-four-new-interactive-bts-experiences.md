Google Gemini and BTS are teaming up to highlight new ways to use Gemini. Starting today, users around the world can explore four new interactive BTS features directly inside the Gemini app, available for a limited time in select languages.
1
The BTS features provide a space to explore real-world prompts from BTS members, learn languages, follow every BTS moment, and bring creative ideas to life.
To mark the launch, we released a YouTube film,
BTS & Gemini | It starts with you
, with a look at how BTS members RM, JIN, SUGA, j-hope, JIMIN, V, and Jung Kook use Gemini in daily life. The film explores how being curious and asking questions sparks creativity and continuous learning, whether you’re organizing travel plans, taking up a new hobby, or bridging language and cultural gaps using the Gemini app.
Ready to try it out? Open the Gemini app on your phone or desktop and type "Unlock BTS" or "7777" in the prompt box. Here are 4 things you can do:
Reveal what BTS asks Gemini
Discover real-world Gemini prompts from BTS members and the creative ideas they’re exploring — from RM’s cultural travel exploration to Jungkook’s passion for bikes.
Guess the BTS song
Looking for the ultimate morning pick-me-up or a relaxing track to unwind?
Guess the BTS song
recommends the perfect BTS song through a fun emoji quiz. Powered by Gemini, the app pairs tracks with custom emoji clues matching the mood and lyrics — leaving it to you to test your BTS knowledge and guess the song.
Follow along at every tour stop
Stay up to date with official news and tour highlights wherever you are in the world. Gemini brings you a daily digest of trending news from the past 48–72 hours, alongside a two-week schedule automatically synced to your local time zone.
Learn everyday Korean
Created for global fans looking to connect more deeply with the music and culture,
Learn Korean with BTS
teaches practical Korean phrases commonly used in fan letters, online comments, and everyday conversation. Complete with clear audio pronunciation guides, it offers an interactive way to practice speaking and writing.
