Starting today, Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can use the "Take notes for me" feature in Google Meet on web and mobile devices in
select languages
. The tool handles note-taking during calls with your permission, allowing you to stay focused on the discussion.
With "Take notes for me," Gemini works in the background to give you:
Real-time capturing:
Gemini transcribes the conversation and creates a meeting summary with key action items.
Seamless saving:
Your notes are automatically saved to a Google Doc in your Google Drive.
Instant recaps:
After the meeting, you’ll get an email with the summary and action items.
Whether you’re tracking next steps during a neighborhood meeting or gathering client requests for a catering order, “Take notes for me” keeps you organized.
If you’re a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscriber — or an eligible Workspace business customer — you can use the feature now for meetings that you host.
During a call:
Click the pencil icon at the top of your Meet window to start note-taking.
For all calls:
Enable the feature under Meeting records in your Google Meet settings.
All meeting participants will be notified when the feature is enabled.
