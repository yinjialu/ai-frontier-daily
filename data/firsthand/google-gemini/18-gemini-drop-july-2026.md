Find out what’s new in the Gemini app in July's Gemini Drop.
Jul 31, 2026
Welcome to the July edition of
Gemini Drops
. See what’s new in our latest Drop:
Speak to Gemini on
macOS
. Create, edit, and summarize by speaking into any active window: Dictate clean text, transform highlighted content, or generate visuals right where your cursor is.
Gemini Spark
is going global. Get things done 24/7, even after closing your laptop, now available worldwide.
1
Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite
. Our latest Flash models are available now with better reasoning and greater speed.
Add yourself to any image. Create content that looks like you by adding your avatar — no need to upload your image every time.
Help that works across your apps: Dropbox, Zillow Rentals, and Viator. Link your favorite apps to Gemini and get more done with a single prompt.
Generate images tailored to you. Use Gemini to create
deeply personalized images
based on your unique interests and preferences. Now available to all users in the US.
Find out even more on the
Gemini Drops Hub
.
1
Gemini Spark is not available in European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Nigeria.
