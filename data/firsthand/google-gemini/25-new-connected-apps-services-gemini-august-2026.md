Now you can connect even more of your favorite apps and services to Gemini.
Mai Lowe
Group Product Manager, Gemini App
Aug 12, 2026
Today at Made by Google, we’re announcing that a
new slate
of connected apps are coming to Gemini to help you get more done. By turning on your favorite services in Gemini, you can easily plan, create, and tackle your to-do list all in one place.
Over the next few weeks, we’re rolling out the ability to connect with tools across a variety of categories:
Productivity and creativity:
Summarize meetings and edit your website with Granola, Otter.ai, and Wix.
Local and entertainment:
Book local experiences, rent a car, reserve your next dinner, and search for event tickets with Fever, GetYourGuide, Localiza, OpenTable (UK), and Ticketmaster.
Music:
Discover new stations and stream your favorite playlists with iHeartRadio and Pandora.
Home, health, and lifestyle:
Find a home professional with Angi or Thumbtack or book a doctor’s appointment with Zocdoc.
